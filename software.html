One Connected System

From maintenance requirement to completed engineering evidence.

Maintenance information is often spread across spreadsheets, paper records, manuals, inboxes and individual engineers. CHMS is designed to connect those activities around the asset.

Managers can define and schedule maintenance, engineers can complete work at the machine, and the resulting history, findings, meter readings, downtime and costs remain visible to the people responsible for reliability.

Platform Objective Make maintenance easier to plan, easier to carry out and easier to prove.

Built Around Industrial Reality

Maintenance schedules have to work around the real site calendar.

CHMS supports more than a simple recurring date. Maintenance can be planned against calendar intervals, operating meters or hybrid requirements while the site's working days, bank holidays and closure periods remain part of the schedule.

CALENDAR Daily to annual maintenance
METER Operating-hour or usage intervals
HYBRID Whichever requirement becomes due first
SITE CALENDAR Working days, bank holidays and shutdowns

Beyond the Core Platform

Focused digital solutions can still be built where the workflow requires it.

CHMS is the main maintenance platform. Where a manufacturing site has a specific engineering workflow that falls outside the core product, additional digital tools or integrations can be considered around the real requirement.

The principle remains the same: understand the engineering process first, then use technology only where it creates a practical improvement.
