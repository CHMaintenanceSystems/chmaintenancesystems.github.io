Why We Exist

Better maintenance starts with understanding the whole system.

CH Maintenance Systems was built around a simple principle: industrial maintenance should be practical, reliable and continually improving.

Our experience comes from working directly with industrial machinery, electrical systems, automation and production environments. This provides an understanding of the pressures faced by modern manufacturers, where downtime can have a significant impact on productivity, cost and delivery.

Rather than approaching maintenance as simply a response to failure, we look at the wider system. Equipment, processes, maintenance strategies, data and the people operating them all have a role to play in achieving reliable production.

CH Maintenance Systems combines this practical engineering understanding with modern digital technology to develop systems that make maintenance more structured, measurable and effective.

Our Focus Engineering solutions that improve reliability, not simply repair failures.

Building What Comes Next

Industrial maintenance is changing. Connected equipment, digital information and intelligent software are creating new opportunities to understand machinery and manage reliability in ways that were previously difficult to achieve.

We believe the next generation of maintenance systems should do more than record what has happened. They should help engineers understand what is happening, identify opportunities for improvement and make better decisions.

This is where CH Maintenance Systems aims to make a difference. By combining engineering experience with software development, we are working towards systems that can fundamentally improve the way industrial maintenance is organised and delivered.

The goal is not technology for the sake of technology. It is technology that solves genuine engineering problems and provides measurable value to the people using it.
