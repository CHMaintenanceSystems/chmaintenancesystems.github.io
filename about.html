Why We Exist

Better maintenance starts with understanding the whole system.

CH Maintenance Systems was built around a simple principle: industrial maintenance should be practical, reliable and continually improving.

Our experience comes from working directly with industrial machinery, electrical systems, automation and production environments. This provides an understanding of the pressures faced by modern manufacturers, where downtime can have a significant impact on productivity, cost and delivery.

Rather than approaching maintenance as simply a response to failure, we look at the wider system. Equipment, processes, maintenance strategies, data and the people operating them all have a role to play in achieving reliable production.

CH Maintenance Systems combines this practical engineering understanding with modern digital technology, including the CHMS maintenance platform, to make maintenance more structured, measurable and effective.

Our Focus Engineering solutions that improve reliability, not simply repair failures.
